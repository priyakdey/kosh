What counts as an expense?

Expenses are money that actually left your bank and is gone. UPI payments, cash, CC bill payments, direct debits for utilities — all expenses.

CC bill payment UPI / Cash Utility bills Rent / Subscriptions
CC swipes → Liabilities Loan EMIs → Liabilities SIPs → Assets Insurance → Protection

Swiped your credit card at a restaurant? That's not an expense yet — it created a liability under your CC. When you pay the CC bill next month, that's the expense. Similarly, EMIs reduce debt (Liabilities), SIPs grow your portfolio (Assets), and premiums buy coverage (Protection).
