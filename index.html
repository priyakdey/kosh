How it works

Honest setup,
honest tracking.

No shortcuts, no pretending. The picture forms as you build it.

01

Sign in with Google

One click. No passwords, no OTPs. We only ask for your email and name — never your bank password.

02

Log what flows through your life

Add income when it arrives. Add expenses when they happen. Or — when available — import from documents you already have: CAS statements, UAN passbooks, bank CSVs.

03

Invite your family when ready

Send email invites. They sign in with Google. You all see the household view together.

04

Watch the picture form

One week tells you nothing. Three months starts telling a story. A year in, you'll see patterns in your household's financial life you never noticed. That's the whole point.

Got questions?

Kosh is a financial OS built for Indian households. It brings together income, expenses, investments (mutual funds, stocks, FDs, PPF, NPS, gold), liabilities, insurance, net worth, and goals — for you individually or with your family.

Today: income and expense tracking, household mode with family invites. Coming: investments (MF, stocks, FDs, PPF/EPF/NPS, SGB), liabilities, insurance, net worth dashboard, FIRE calculator, and goals. Every feature is marked clearly on the features section — we don't hide the roadmap.

Not a broker. We don't let you buy or sell anything. Use your broker for that; bring the data here.

Not an advisor. No one at Kosh recommends investments. We show you the numbers; you decide.

Not a Splitwise for roommates. No "who owes whom" logic. This is for families sharing a life, not a rent.

Not instant gratification. Tracking takes effort. Insight compounds over months. We won't pretend otherwise.

INDmoney and most aggregators focus on investments. Zerodha Console only shows your Zerodha holdings. Spreadsheets don't know PPF from NPS and certainly don't handle your spouse's data. Kosh is the whole household picture — not just one slice, not just your data, and designed specifically for Indian financial instruments.

Yes — but we'd rather you didn't.

The founder's view: households that share a life share a financial reality. Every rupee you track should show up in the household number. Surprises, personal allowances, pre-marital assets — we think all of it belongs in the shared picture, because that's what a household is. A tool that makes it easy to quietly park meaningful money outside the household total isn't helping you manage money together; it's helping you have a different conversation than the one you should probably be having.

The product's view: you might disagree, and you're an adult. So when you add an entry, there's a small checkbox — mark as personal. Tick it and that entry stays in your Personal view only; it won't appear in Family view, won't affect household totals, won't be visible to your partner. No dark patterns, no friction to find it — just a checkbox, clearly labeled.

We're telling you exactly what we believe and exactly how the product works. Use it however fits your household. We'd rather be honest about the tension than hedge.

Encrypted in transit and at rest. Google sign-in only — we never see a password. We don't sell, share, or monetize your financial data. Your data is yours; you can export or delete it at any time.

Not currently — Kosh is manual-entry today. You can import from documents you already own (CAS statements, UAN passbooks, bank CSVs) as those features roll out. We're evaluating India's Account Aggregator framework for the future, but we'll never ask you for bank passwords directly.

You invite family members by email. They sign in with their own Google account and get their own personal portfolio. The household portfolio combines everyone's shared data. Personal stays personal; household is shared. Each member tracks their own numbers; the household view sums the picture.
