Kosh is a personal and household finance tracker purpose-built for India. It lets you track your income, expenses, assets (mutual funds, stocks, FDs, PPF, NPS, gold, real estate), liabilities (loans, credit cards), insurance policies, and financial goals — all in one place. Think of it as your complete financial operating system.

Yes. Kosh is currently in early access and completely free — no hidden charges, no credit card required. We'll introduce paid plans in the future for premium features, but early access users will always have access to the core features at no cost.

Most finance apps are built for western markets and bolt on Indian support as an afterthought. Kosh is built India-first — we natively support PPF, NPS, EPF, SGB, SCSS, FDs, RDs, and Indian mutual fund schemes. Amounts display in lakhs and crores, not millions. And our household model understands how Indian families actually manage money together.

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Kosh never connects to your bank accounts or investment platforms — you add data manually, which means we never have access to your credentials. Your financial data stays yours.
